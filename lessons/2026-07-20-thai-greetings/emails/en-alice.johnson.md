Hi Alice,

สวัสดีค่ะ (sà-wàt-dii khâ) — go on, read it out loud. That is you saying "hello" in Thai, and you can already do it.

What a first lesson! Today we covered the everyday greeting สวัสดี, introducing yourself with ฉันชื่อ… (no "to be" verb needed — ชื่อ already means "to be named"), asking someone's name, and a warm goodbye. We also met the two big beginner truths: Thai has 5 tones, and for English speakers the "puffed vs unpuffed" consonants (ป vs พ) are actually trickier than the tones.

Your 5 key words:

| Thai | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| สวัสดี | sà-wàt-dii | hello / goodbye |
| ค่ะ | khâ | polite particle (women) |
| ชื่อ | chʉ̂ʉ | name / to be named |
| ขอบคุณ | khɔ̀ɔp-khun | thank you |
| แล้วเจอกัน | láeo jəə gan | see you later |

Sentence of the day — say it to the mirror:
ฉันชื่อแอลิสค่ะ — chǎn chʉ̂ʉ Alice khâ — "My name is Alice."

You are doing wonderfully — starting is the hardest part, and you have already done it. เก่งมาก! (gèng mâak, "well done!"). For today, these three lines are genuinely enough for a real first exchange:
1. สวัสดีค่ะ — "Hello."
2. ฉันชื่อ…ค่ะ — "My name is…"
3. ขอบคุณค่ะ — "Thank you."
